# User guide and quick start (USR-01)

## Get started

1. Open the app address. Choose **Create a business**. Give the business name, an address (letters, numbers, hyphens), your name, email, a password, and a **manager PIN** (4 to 8 digits). Tick *Start with a sample menu* to try it with example items.
2. Next time, **Sign in** with the business address, email and password.
3. The strip at the top always shows whether the books balance. Green means every check passes. Tap it to see the checks.

## Take an order (Sell)

- Tap **New bill** (optionally type a table), then tap menu items to add them. Use **+ / −** to change quantity before the kitchen has made the item.
- Tap **Made** when the kitchen has prepared an item. After that, removing it needs a manager's PIN and a reason.
- Take payment: type the amount received and tap **Cash**, **M-Pesa** or **Card**. A bill with nothing owing closes by itself.

## When a customer cannot pay now

Tap **Close without full payment…** and choose:

| Choice | Use when | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Put on customer credit | A regular customer will pay later | The customer. A manager PIN only if over their limit |
| Deduct from staff | A staff member owes it | Staff member and a manager PIN |
| Complimentary | On the house | Reason and a manager PIN |
| Write off | It will never be collected | Reason and a manager PIN |

The manager must be a **different person** from the one at the till.

## Voiding a bill

**Void bill…** sends a request. A manager approves it under **Stock → Approvals**. Bills with payments cannot be voided.

## Stock

- **On hand** shows quantity and value per store. Negative stock shows in red: sales went through before the delivery was recorded.
- **Stock take:** choose a store and count what is on the shelf. You will not see what the system expects. Save, then **Submit for approval**. A different person approves.
- **Wastage:** pick the item, store, exact quantity and a reason. Entries dated more than 2 days back wait for approval.
- **Move stock** between stores, and record **consumables** (straws, tissue, cleaning materials).

## Buy

Choose the supplier, the store and the **date the goods arrived**. Add stock lines (with the unit, e.g. carton) and cost lines (repairs, equipment). Equipment and repairs are **not stock**: put them under the right expense or asset line. Save, then **Pay supplier** when you pay.

## Money

- **Day end:** the checklist shows what is still open. **Close the day** when everything is clear.
- **Cash:** count the till and enter it; any difference is recorded. **Bank cash** records a deposit.
- **Expenses:** record what you paid and how.
- **Debtors and staff:** record payments from customers and staff who owe.

## Reports

Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, customers who owe, suppliers you owe. Use the date boxes and **Apply dates**.

## Good habits

Record things when they happen. Close every bill properly. Count stock on a schedule. Never keep a paper record that the system does not have.

## New in 0.2

- **Receipts and kitchen tickets:** on an open bill, **Kitchen ticket** prints only what has not gone to the kitchen yet. **Receipt** prints the bill.
- **Tips:** type the tip next to the amount when taking payment. Tips are held for staff, not counted as sales.
- **Service charge:** **Service charge…** adds a percentage to the bill. Press it again after changing items.
- **Other ways to pay:** customer deposit, gift card or loyalty points (the customer must be on the bill for deposits and points).
- **Refunds:** open the **Paid** tab, choose the bill, **Refund an item…**. A manager must approve. Say whether the item goes back on the shelf; if not, its cost is recorded as wastage.
- **More** (owner, manager, accountant): **Today** (sales, things to watch, one-tap approvals), **Hotel** (rooms, reservations, folios, night audit), **Staff and pay** (employees, payroll, leave), **Trade** (who owes you, statements, orders, price lists, promotions), **Planning** (what to order), **Import**, **Settings**, **Plan and billing**, **Outlets**.
- **Outlet picker** (top of the page, if you have more than one outlet): shows one outlet's bills, stock and numbers.
- **Install on your phone:** open the app in the phone's browser and choose "Add to home screen". It still needs an internet connection.
- **Free Books Health Check** is on the sign-in page: upload exports from your old system and see what is leaking. No account needed.
